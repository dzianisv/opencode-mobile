# Brand — OpenCode Mobile

Created 2026-11-18 (Product Hunt launch prep, PR growth/product-hunt-launch).
All values sampled from the shipped product, not invented.

## Mark

- Source: `assets/icon.png` (1024x1024, the shipped app icon). The mark is
  a `>_` terminal prompt in blue on dark navy, with the wordmark
  "opencode" below it. No tagline inside the mark.
- The Product Hunt thumbnail is the same mark, downscaled to 240x240
  (`growth/product-hunt/thumbnail-240x240.png`).

## Colors

- Navy background: `#0F172A` (icon background, most common pixel)
- Blue accent: `#3B82F6` (icon mark and border)
- Wordmark grey-blue: `#64748B`
- Subtext on navy: `#94A3B8`

## Tagline

"Your AI coding agent, in your pocket" (36 characters, under the 60-char
Product Hunt limit). Same promise as the Play short description
("AI coding agent in your pocket"). One tagline everywhere — if it
changes, every card changes with it.

## Style

**Precise.** Flat dark navy, one blue accent, large plain type, real
screenshots. No gradients, no decoration, no stock art. A dev tool that
looks like a dev tool.

## Voice

Plain, direct, developer-to-developer. No hype. Say what it does, what it
costs (free), and what it never touches (your server, your keys).
